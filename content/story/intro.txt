# The story intro (ticket 31): twelve comic panels, 1920 x 1080 each. The editor's StoryCapture (31.5) reads this file, poses the stage,
# renders, draws the comic devices and letters each panel into Assets/Story/Panels/panel-NN.png. Read by SDFPipeline/Story/StoryFile.cs
# (the grammar is documented there). 42 (Mushroom 42, the red amanita scout) is "they", Chanterelle Prime is "he".
#
# Frames of reference
#   Lettering: balloon (a character speaks, its tail at a spot or an actor), sky (Prime's jagged box), caption (the narrator, wide and low),
#   note (the narrator's small square side note beside a character, its arrow at=(x, y) or at="actor"; it names who they are, as the balloons do not), sfx.
#   Stage coordinates are the pipeline's, in metres, as content/scenes/home.txt (the capture mirrors x for Unity): the room's floor top is
#   y = 0, x -2..2, z -1.5..1.5, the back wall (z = -1.5) with the window; the meadow is content/levels/meadow.level (red castle at x = -62,
#   red flag stand (-60.5, 0), red spawn (-52, 20) facing +x). The warp stage is empty: the camera sits at the origin looking along +z.
#   rot is (pitch, yaw, roll) in degrees: yaw counter-clockwise from above with 0 facing +z (90 faces +x, 180 faces the back wall), pitch
#   positive looks (or tips the front of an actor) down. Frame-space points (anchor, tail, at) are 0..1 from the frame's top left.
#
# Actors
#   Home stage: "Mushroom 42" (sits on the chair, back to us), "Mushroom 7", "Chanterelle Hand 1".."Chanterelle Hand 4" (parked off stage)
#   as home.txt names them; props by their model name ("chair", "snack-bowl", "monitor"). Meadow and warp stages: the scouts exist only
#   where a panel names them. A line without part= poses the actor as a whole, absolute; with part= it changes one part of it from its rest
#   pose, in the actor's own space (offset, extra rotation, scale factor). The scouts' parts are body, eye-left, eye-right, hand-left and
#   hand-right; a mirrored part is seen from the actor's own front, so at yaw 180 the actor's left is the stage's +x. The chair's is seat,
#   the monitor's screen, the snack bowl's crisp_1 .. crisp_6.
