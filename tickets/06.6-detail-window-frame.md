# 06.6 — Detail piece: window frame

Part of [06](06-castles.md) step 6; rules in [06.0](06.0-rules.md). A small instanced part; placement is 06.10.

Depends on: nothing.

## Goal

`content/parts/castle/castle_window_frame.sdf`, modelled at its own origin, base at y = 0, facing +z, in the castle's mushroom style: a
rounded bracket-fungus frame (thick lip, 8 to 12 cm deep) round a round-topped window about 0.5 m wide and 0.8 m high, the opening
filled by a **glowing pane** (a thin disc or slab in a glowing material: see how `castle_keep.sdf`'s round windows glow, and use the
same). Frame in `amanita_stem` or a cream bracket tone from the existing materials. The opening is painted, not cut: the pane is a
flat inset of 1 to 2 cm (06's rule for small windows).
Header lines: `collider none`, `shadows off`, `cull 35`, `outline off`. Choose `Detail detail=` so LOD0 is at most 300 triangles at 128 cells.

## Files

`content/parts/castle/castle_window_frame.sdf` only.

## Commands

A scratch model that uses the part once (in the scratchpad, or temporarily in the repo and not committed): `sdf mesh scratch.sdf <out>
--resolution 128 --lods 3` for triangle counts per level; `sdf render` front and three-quarter views and look at them.
`dotnet build`, `dotnet test`.

## Acceptance

- The part loads, meshes within the budget, reads as a window in the render, and glows.
- Report the triangle counts per level.
