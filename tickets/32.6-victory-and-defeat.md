Subticket of [32 — Campaign and game flow](32-campaign-flow.md). Goal: victory and Defeat, as the sections of ticket 32 describe.

The match end: time slows to 0.3 for 1 s, a 2 s banner ("RED WINS!" / "BLUE WINS!"), then `Victory` or `Defeat`; Round End goes.
Victory: banner, arena, score, medal with NEW BEST, captures, kills, deaths, time; CONTINUE (next step; from a Level Select replay back
to Level Select), RETRY, MENU. Defeat: DEFEATED in blue, one of Prime's taunts (5 per arena, as data), score, tip; RETRY, RETRY EASIER
(after two losses in a row on the battle), MENU. RETRY resets the match in place with no reload. The death screen gets "Back in 5".
Scoreboard subtitle is the arena and goal. Saving after a win and on a difficulty change (`PlayerPrefs`, `PlayerPrefs.Save()`), the loss
streak kept. Pause: RESTART and QUIT TO MENU confirm via `Confirm`.
