# 27.5 — Models: tall blockers, the track and the train

Goal: the tall toys, the track pieces and the train cars of 27's tables. Same conventions as 27.4 (`content/models/toys/`, `materials/toys/`, 60x scale,
palette, team colours out, a comment at each file's head, png looked at, bounds tests, 256 cells and 3 LODs).

Models (sizes, colliders and budgets from 27's "Tall blockers", "The loop" and "New models list"):
- `alpha-block0..2` (one, two, three alphabet blocks, stacked, tilted or leaning, painted wood in the palette, letters raised or painted), `duplo-tower0..1`
  (large studded bricks stepped, no brand marks), `skittle0..1`, `spinning-top0` (tin, tilted on its rim).
- `track-curve0`: a 30° arc of wooden track on a 20 m radius, 2.4 m wide, 0.3 m high, collider none, origin so that twelve of them at 30° yaw steps about a
  circle centre make a ring; say in the file's head which point is the origin and where the arc starts and ends. Sleepers and two rails in simple forms.
- `track-bridge0`: 24 m long along z; 8 m ramps (25°) up to an 8 m deck at 3.8 m, an arch beneath 6 m wide and 3.2 m clear for the road, wooden trestle look,
  2.4 m wide track on top, collider mesh (ramps and deck walkable; side rails only if they cannot trap a player, else none).
- `toy-train-engine` (4.8 × 1.8 × 2.6, mint green body, yellow wheels, white chimney), `toy-train-flat` (flat wagon, lilac deck at 1.0 m, rideable),
  `toy-train-wagon` (open wagon, peach, 1.8 m sides), convex colliders, couplings so a 15 m train reads as one. Each car's origin is its centre on the track,
  +z the direction of travel, wheels' bottoms on y = 0 (the mover raises the car).

Check: `dotnet test` passes; a test shows twelve curves close a ring (arc end and start points against the origin note). Acceptance: all rendered and looked at,
within budget (27's table), tests pass. Dependencies: none.
