# 06.3 — Shader pattern stage and distance fade

Part of [06](06-castles.md) step 5; rules in [06.0](06.0-rules.md). Draws the pattern per pixel, in the part's own space, and fades it
with distance.

Depends on: nothing to write (UV7 is empty until 06.2, and kind 0 means no pattern); seen working in 06.5.

## Goal

New `Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Shaders/SdfPattern.hlsl` and a call in `SdfToon.shader`'s fragment, between `SdfToonColor` and
`SdfToonShade`. Per-vertex input `uv7` = (kind, size, grout, weight); kind 0 returns at once. Kinds: stones (courses of blocks in running
bond, per-block colour jitter, a bevelled normal near the grout, ink in the grout), fibres, gills. Lines are ink: the colour times
`_ToonInk` (a new material property with a default, so the shader works before 06.2).

**Distance fade**: lines thin out and blend to the material's mean colour from 25 to 40 m (where LOD1 takes over), and sooner where
`fwidth` says a line would be under a pixel and a half, so there is no shimmer and no pop at LOD switches.

**Merge notes (ticket 10)**: the fragment must keep the `glow` argument of `SdfToonShade` and the final `SkyFade(color, input.positionWS)`.
Do not touch those lines: only insert the pattern call and carry `uv7` through `Attributes`, `Varyings` and `Vert`. The instanced variant
(`SDF_SCATTER`) shares this fragment. Count the interpolators already used and stay within WebGL2's limit (16 vectors; the attempt used
TEXCOORD10). The outline shader is untouched; `SdfToonFar.shader` gets no pattern.

## Reuse

`06-new/SdfPattern.hlsl` (a complete draft) and the `SdfToon.shader` hunk of the patch. Review the draft: it uses `SdfHash` and
`SdfNoise` (check they exist in `SdfSurface.hlsl`), `TransformWorldToObject` under GPU instancing and `fwidth` terms. Make it compile
cleanly and keep ALU modest (no loops, no textures).

## Files

`Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Shaders/SdfPattern.hlsl` (+ `.meta` as Unity makes it), `SdfToon.shader`.

## Commands

Unity batch `BuildAllInBatch` (compiles the shaders); the log has no shader errors, including for the `SDF_SCATTER` variant.

## Acceptance

- Compiles with no errors or warnings new to this change; `glow` and `SkyFade` still in the fragment (show the final lines).
- Meshes without UV7 render as before.
