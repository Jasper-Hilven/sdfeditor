Goal: the models of the `toy` machine set (27.2 names it): per team (red, blue), in `content/models/machines/` with team materials in `materials/machines/`. Read
`tickets/done/24-machines.md` (24.1) and the existing `turret-<team>-*`, `tank-*`, `helicopter-*` files and their tests; copy the part structure, pivots and the
floating eyes (the scouts' eye part, two per machine, children of the turning part) exactly.

- `turret-toy-<team>-foot/head/barrel`: a wind-up tin soldier about 2 m: round tin stand with a key in its back (foot), torso, arms and tall hat (head, turns), a toy
  rifle (barrel, tilts); tunic in the team colour, white belts.

LOD0 totals: turret 5k, tank 4k, helicopter 4k. Tests as 24.1 (bounds: tank and helicopter within 0.8 m, turret about 2 m; the parts present under the same names).
Render and look at every png. Mesh at 256 with 3 LODs and report the counts.
