# 27.6 — Models: the landmarks

Goal: the big toys of 27's "Landmarks" table as models:
- `jack-in-the-box0`: 5 m painted box, yellow and lilac, lid open, a spring and a jester (green and peach, bells, painted toy face) to 11 m, 22k.
- `block-tower0`: a leaning tower of alphabet blocks, two blocks missing at 5 m to shoot through, 6 × 6 × 10.5, 18k.
- `rocking-horse0`: cream with a peach mane, 12 × 4 × 9, belly at 4 m, rockers 0.8 m, 20k.
- `ring-stacker0`: five rings mint to lilac on a cone peg, 8 across, 11 tall, 15k.
- `ferris-wheel0`: 12 m wheel of spokes, six gondolas, an A-frame whose legs leave 4 m or nothing between them, 12 × 4 × 14, 25k.
- `teddy0`: slumped against a wall, legs out, honey-brown, one ear chewed, painted bead eyes, felt and a button, 13 × 9 × 16, 25k.
All collider mesh, 60x scale, palette and conventions as 27.4.

Steps: write the .sdf files (shared shapes into `content/parts/toys/`), materials in `materials/toys/` (painted wood, plush, felt, cloth, spring tin, button),
`sdf render` each and LOOK at the png from several sides; each must read from 100 m as what it is (a silhouette). No red or blue groups (the teddy's honey brown is
not red). Bounds tests in `SDFPipeline.Tests/Models/`; the openings (the tower's gap, the horse's belly, the wheel's frame) checked by a test or the render. Mesh at
256 with 3 LODs and report the LOD0 counts.

Check: `dotnet test`. Acceptance: all six rendered and looked at, within budget, tests pass. Dependencies: none.

Common rules (every subticket of 27): read `tickets/27-toy-box-level.md`, `tickets/README.md` and `tickets/done/26-level-ruleset.md` first. Work only in this
worktree (the absolute path the lead gives you), never the main checkout. Meshing always at 256 cells, 3 LODs (castle parts 128), never 512. Each SDF pointer
id is one node (reuse only via variables). One theme per scene: toys; no mushroom features on props. Toon look only, no live raymarching, nothing that needs
compute shaders (WebGL2). Team colours stay out of props: no red or blue toy groups (check each png). Generated files (glbs, mesh assets, level bakes) are not
in git. Match surrounding code style and comment density. Do not commit; the lead commits. Do not spawn agents. 27's open questions are answered with its
defaults: the train pushes and does 20 damage, riding is in, the machines keep the scouts' floating eyes, the teddy and jester have painted toy faces. Report:
files changed, commands run, test results (failures stated honestly), what is left for a human look.
