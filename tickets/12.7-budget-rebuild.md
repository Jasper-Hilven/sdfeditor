dropped or rescaled where over.

## Budget

High preset: about 2M triangles in view; web preset: about 500k triangles and at most 200 draw calls (11 measured 143k at the
busiest eye-height crowd shot for the chess pieces alone). Measure from eye height at the busiest game-piece spots, from a corner
across the whole board, and from above. Draw calls: count the renderers of the placed game pieces; just report their share (the
chess pieces' draw calls are 11's business).

## If over budget

Drop or rescale game pieces in the level file (largest triangle contributors first: hotels, `card-house`, chip stacks), keep all 20
models used if possible, run 12.6's overlap test (`dotnet test`), rebake, rebuild, re-measure.

## Acceptance

- Unity batch runs with no `error CS`, shader error or exception; `Chessboard.unity` rebuilt and committed.
- Result section: triangles for each view on both presets (placed models, ground), renderer count of the game pieces, and what was
  dropped or rescaled.
- `dotnet build` and `dotnet test` pass.
