# 06.10 — Place the detail pieces in the castle

Part of [06](06-castles.md) step 6; rules in [06.0](06.0-rules.md). Uses the pieces of 06.6 to 06.9 in the layout.

Depends on: 06.6, 06.7, 06.8, 06.9 (the part files); 06.4 (regenerate castle0.glb on top of it).

## Goal

In `content/models/buildings/castle0.sdf`, `Part` uses of the four pieces, under translates and turns like the existing details:
- **window frames**: on the keep, the towers and the gate turrets, a few per building, at heights that clear the wall-walk rings
  (5 m, 5.15 m, 5.3 m: see the floor-height comment in the file) and the stairs; about 12 to 20 in all;
- **wall shelf fungi**: on outer wall faces and at the foot of towers, about 10 to 16, varied in turn and height;
- **lanterns**: by the gate (two), on the keep's ring and on a few tower rings, about 6 to 10;
- **veils**: one or two on the keep's ring, one on each of two towers, about 4.

Nothing across a doorway, the stairs or the wall-walk; nothing inside the walkable volume (the player is 0.3 m wide and 1.75 m high;
the pieces have no collider, but must not look wrong from the wall-walk). Keep the file's style (comments naming the groups).
Regenerate `castle0.glb` at 128 cells with 3 LODs; the commit holds the layout and the glb. Do not rebuild the Unity assets here (06.11).

## Files

`content/models/buildings/castle0.sdf`, `Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Generated/buildings/castle0.glb`.

## Commands

As [06.4](06.4-castle-materials.md) for the mesh. `sdf render` the castle: front, three-quarter, and 1.5 m views from the field and from
the courtyard; look at them. `dotnet build`, `dotnet test`.

## Acceptance

- The glb has one mesh per detail kind and one node per use; they are instances (no "part meshed in place" warning for the new pieces).
- Renders show the pieces where intended, none floating or buried. The castle's LOD0 total stays under +15 % of the 91k in 06's Status;
  report the new total and the per-piece counts.
