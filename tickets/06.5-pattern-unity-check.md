# 06.5 — Patterns in the engine: look, fade, cost

Part of [06](06-castles.md) step 5; rules in [06.0](06.0-rules.md). Seeing it work and tuning it.

Depends on: 06.2, 06.3, 06.4.

## Goal

Rebuild the prefabs and the meadow in Unity with the patterned castle, look at it, tune the pattern scales and the fade, commit the
rebuilt assets.

## Work

1. `BuildAllInBatch`; commit the changed `Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Meshes/*` and `Unity/Assets/Level/**` assets.
2. Snapshots with `LevelSnapshot.Capture` (far end of the field, 28 m before the gate, at the gate, courtyard, wall-walk, and a close
   shot of a bay and a tower from 1.5 m) and `MushroomSnapshot.Capture` for castle0 at LOD0, 1 and 2. Put them in
   `.claude/jobs/06-5-snaps/` of your worktree (not committed) and look at them.
3. Check: blocks and grout read as drawn (ink line, a bevel catching a band); no shimmer (compare two snapshots a few cm apart at 20,
   30 and 40 m); no visible change across the LOD0/1 switch; gills and fibres sit on the right surfaces (gills under caps and on
   shelves, fibres on stems and not on rings); the night preset still glows (the `glow` argument works).
4. Adjust the sizes in the three `.sdfmat` and constants in `SdfPattern.hlsl` if needed. Re-mesh castle0 at 128 if a material changed.
5. Cost: count the pattern's ALU from the code and note it for 06's Status (web preset).

## Acceptance

- No errors in the Unity log. The report describes the snapshots: what reads well, what was tuned.
- The checks pass, or the failure is stated exactly (what, where, at which distance).
