# 16 — Weapon pickup pods

A weapon lies on a **pod**: a low, round pedestal 30 cm tall that the player walks onto, with the weapon floating over its middle,
1 m over the pod's top, turning at 6 revolutions per minute. Walking into it takes the weapon, and the floating weapon disappears
until it respawns. One pod prefab per weapon model, generated by an editor builder, so a new weapon gets its pod without hand work.

Depends on: the weapon models (`content/models/weapons/`) and their prefabs; no inventory is needed (the game has none, see below).

## The pod model

`content/models/pickups/weapon-pod.sdf`, with `materials/pickups/pod_stone.sdfmat` and `pod_glow.sdfmat`.

- A cone-shaped pedestal, foot radius 1.3 m, flat top radius 0.95 m at **y = 0.3 m**: the edge is a bevel of about 40 degrees, under
  the player's slope limit (45) and with no step in it, so the first-person controller walks up it (its step offset, 0.4 m, would
  take it anyway). The top edge is rounded a little, as props are.
- A **glow ring** (mint-teal, strong subsurface so it stays bright in the shade) is inlaid in the top at 0.55 to 0.68 m from the
  middle, its top 1 cm under the stone's: a groove in the stone, filled with the glow material.
- `collider convex`: the hull of a cone is the cone, and the pod has no hollow to walk into. Planes are not used (the renderer's
  framing leaves infinite shapes out): boxes cut the top and the foot.
- Toon look like every prop: one `SdfToon` material from the vertices and the ink outline from the builder. Meshed at 256 with 3 LODs
  (about 3.5k, 0.8k and 0.3k triangles).

## Unity: `Assets/Pickups/`

- `Runtime/SpinHover.cs`: on the weapon's pivot. Serialized `revolutionsPerMinute` (6), `hoverHeight` (1, metres over the pod's top),
  `baseHeight` (the pod's top, set by the builder from the pod's mesh, 0.3), and a small bob (`bobAmplitude` 3 cm, `bobPeriod` 3 s;
  set the amplitude to 0 for none).
- `Runtime/WeaponPickup.cs`: on the pod's root, which also holds the trigger (an upright capsule, radius 0.9 m, over the pod).
  **The game has no player inventory or pickup logic**: `FirstPersonController` only walks; `GuiHud` has a `pickupPrompt` fed by the
  fake match's `m.pickup` string, and `TracerGun` and the other effect guns are showcase effects. So this is a minimal component:
  anything with a `CharacterController` that enters the trigger takes the weapon; the weapon object is hidden; `onPickedUp`
  (`UnityEvent<string>` with the weapon id) and the static `WeaponPickup.PickedUp` (pod, player object) are raised. The pod
  respawns the weapon after `respawnSeconds` (15; 0 or less for never), also under a player who is still standing on it.
- `Editor/WeaponPodBuilder.cs`: for each weapon `.glb` in the Generated folder's `weapons` group (variants such as `minigun_512`
  count as `minigun`) it writes `Assets/Pickups/Pods/<weapon>-pod.prefab`: `Pod` (the weapon-pod prefab), `Weapon` (`SpinHover`)
  with `Model` (the weapon's prefab at 2.25 times its size, `WeaponScale` (the minigun 1.5, `MinigunScale`), shifted so the middle of its bounds is on the pivot, so it
  turns on the spot). Rebuilt when
  missing, older than the weapon's or the pod's prefab, or built by an older `BuilderVersion`; checked when the editor loads and
  after `MushroomBuilder` built prefabs. `Tools > SDF Pickups > Rebuild Pods`, or in a batch
  `SdfPickups.Editor.WeaponPodBuilder.BuildAllInBatch`; `LevelBuilder.BuildAllInBatch` does it as well.

## Weapons with a pod

blob-gun, electro-gun, flamethrower, minigun, pistol, poison-gun, sniper (every model in `content/models/weapons/`).

## Tests

- `SDFPipeline.Tests/Models/WeaponPodTests.cs`: the pod's bounds are about 0.3 m tall and stand on the ground; the top is flat at
  0.3 m and the bevel's steepest stretch is under the slope limit; the glow ring lies 1 cm under the stone's top.
- `dotnet build` and `dotnet test` pass.

## Not placed in a level

The pods are prefabs only; no level places one yet (that is a level-design choice: `place` lines in `content/levels/*.level` would need
the baked level to know a prefab outside `content/models`, which this ticket does not touch).
