# 29.2 — Models: floor clutter and the ground materials

Goal: the small bathroom things of 29's "Ground clutter" and "New models list" as SDF models with materials, rendered, bounds-tested and meshed. Models in
`content/models/bathroom/`, parts in `content/parts/bathroom/` (if needed), materials in `materials/bathroom/` (palette and rules in 29 "Theme and scale").

Models (sizes, colliders `none`, LOD0 budgets as in 29's tables): `suds0..2` (foam heaps 0.6-1.5 m across, 0.2-0.45 tall), `droplet0..2` (domed drops 0.12-0.3 across, 0.06-0.12
tall), `bubble0` (0.4 m, opaque pearl, rim light shows the shine), `hairpin0..1` (1.5 m, dark bronze), `swab0` (2.25 m), `swab-half0` (1.1 m), `bath-bead0..2` (0.3 m, pearl
yellow, lilac, mint). Origin at the base's centre on y = 0, front along +z. Each file starts with a comment saying what it is and its size in metres. `Detail detail=0.5` on
big simple forms; thin things (hairpins, swabs, drops) must survive 256-cell meshing (27.4 found thin sheets vanish at coarser cells; see `tickets/done/27.4-*.md`).

Ground materials in `materials/ground/` (follow `ground_felt`/`ground_foam` etc. and read the ground material format): `ground_bathroom-tile` (the base: warm white `#F3F0E8`
5 m tiles drawn from the world grid with a grey-mint `#C8D6CF` grout joint; if the base material can draw the square grid by itself the 48 grout strips of 29 are not needed:
decide and say which), `ground_grout` (only if strips stay), `ground_wet` (a shade darker and glossier, grout lost under it), `ground_puddle` (flat pale grey-mint, hard white
highlight band, ripple rings in the shader, no transparency, no reflection), `ground_suds` (smeared white foam film with a bubble pattern, glossy). Names as in 29's "Regions".

Steps: write the .sdf files and materials; `sdf render --style toon` each and LOOK at every png: is it the bathroom thing it should be, any red/blue/pink/sky-blue, any mushroom
look? Fix. Bounds tests in `SDFPipeline.Tests/Models/` (size, base on y = 0). Mesh at 256 cells with 3 LODs and check LOD0 triangle counts against the budgets; list them.

Check: `dotnet test` passes. Acceptance: every model rendered and looked at, within budget (clutter up to 500), tests pass. Dependencies: none.
