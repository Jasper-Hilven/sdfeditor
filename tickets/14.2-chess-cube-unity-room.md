   `RoomChecker` parity across the cube edges (add an editor check sampling the 12 edges). Colliders on all six faces so nothing

## Status

Done (14.2), left for a human look: a Play-mode walk to the walls and a jump at the ceiling, and the lamp's tone by eye.

- **Walls and ceiling**: option 1. `LevelRoom` (`Unity/Assets/Level/Editor/LevelRoom.cs`) builds Level/Room from level.json's `room` block: a flat
  quad per wall and the ceiling facing inward, on `SdfMushrooms/RoomFace` (`Assets/Level/Shaders/SdfRoomFace.shader`), which draws the squares from the
  world position (filtered square wave, so edges are ruled straight and anti-aliased; the light and dark ground materials with their speckle), one
  material per face (`Assets/Level/Materials/<level> Room <face>.mat`). The floor stays the ground. A 4 m thick box collider sits outside each of the six
  faces. The old invisible play-area walls are not built for a level with a room.
- **Edge check**: `LevelRoom.CheckEdges` (run at the end of every room level's build, from Tools > SDF Level > Check Room Edges, and in batch with
  `-executeMethod SdfLevel.Editor.LevelRoom.CheckInBatch`) compares every square of the five drawn faces, as the materials' own values give it,
  with the baked dark rows, and samples all 12 cube edges, one point per square along each, 1 cm either side: colours differ across the floor's and the
  ceiling's 8 edges and match along the 4 vertical wall-to-wall edges (14.1's rule). Passes.
- **No sky**: a level without sky data gets no Level/Sky object, no skybox (`RenderSettings.skybox` null, no sky material made), no sky meshes; only an
  identity `SkySettings` on the Level root (so the toon globals are known, with no distance fade). The camera clears to flat black, which a closed room
  never shows. The stale tracked `chessboard Sky *` materials, `chessboard Sky.meta` and the scene's `Chessboard/Sky.mat` and `Post Processing.asset` are
  removed (`git rm`, not committed).
- **Light** (written in `LevelBuilder.RoomLight`): light only, no lamps and no dome. One directional light overhead, towards (0.3, 1, 0.35), warm white
  (1, 0.95, 0.84), flat dim ambient as the showcase's. Walls and ceiling cast no shadow and receive none; pieces cast and receive them on the floor. A wall
  only grazes an overhead light, so the room shader leans the light direction toward each face's normal (1.0): walls read in the lit band, the ceiling in
  the mid band, the floor lit with the pieces' shadows in the shadow band.
- **Camera**: far plane 450 m in a room (the cube's far corner is 277 m away), no distance fade, so far walls keep their colours. **Player**: the spawn
  (0, 0, 0) and the CharacterController work inside; the colliders keep it in.
- **Snapshots** (`LevelSnapshot`, a room level shoots `room_*` views and the crowd, none of the hills' or sky's): eye height in the middle in four
  directions, along a wall from a corner, diagonally across and up, up at the ceiling (straight and slanted), at a wall's foot, at a wall edge, at the wall
  and ceiling edge, from high up, the crowd, and the web preset. The cube is closed everywhere, the checker runs on across the edges, no sky.
