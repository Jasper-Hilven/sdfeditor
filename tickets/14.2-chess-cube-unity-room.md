Goal: `LevelBuilder` builds the closed cube from the baked room data (14.1) and `Chessboard.unity` shows it.

Steps:
1. Walls and ceiling: option 1 of the ticket (flat quads with a checker drawn in a shader from world position, as `SdfGround.shader`
   draws the floor's squares; same toon look and ink line; square edges straight at eye height). The colours must match 14.1's
   `RoomChecker` parity across the cube edges (add an editor check sampling the 12 edges). Colliders on all six faces so nothing
   can leave the room.
2. No sky: for a level with no sky data, `LevelBuilder`/`LevelSky` builds no sky objects, sky materials or sky meshes; the camera does not
   clear to a sky it never shows. Remove the stale tracked `chessboard Sky *` materials and meshes if they are in git.

   (directional light from overhead, warm white, toon tone), walls and ceiling not casting the directional light's shadows while pieces
   still cast on the floor. Toon bands must read.
4. Camera: far plane and distance fade reach across 160 m and up to the ceiling; the fade must not wash far walls into the meadow's fog colour.
5. The first-person controller's spawn and bounds work inside the room.
