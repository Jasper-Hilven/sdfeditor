# 29.4 — Models: the tub, riser, towels, giant duck and toilet-roll pyramid

Goal: the five biggest landmarks of 29's "Landmarks" and "New models list" tables, with materials. `content/models/bathroom/`, materials `materials/bathroom/`.

- `bathtub0`: 51 × 22.5 × 16.5 m, `collider mesh lod=1`; a built-in white enamel tub with a front panel (no feet), half filled; the water a flat mint-grey surface (never blue)
  with a raft of suds at 8 m; the water creeping over the front rim at the middle in a thin glossy sheet; front face at local +z (the level places it with yaw 180), origin at the
  middle of its floor footprint. 25k LOD0. Enamel, water, foam materials.
- `shower-riser0`: the tub's mixer tap and spout on the wall (y 20-24 m), a riser rail to a round shower head at 57 m (head 4.5 m across, rail 1 m thick, 40 m tall), chrome (pale
  silver, toon hard highlight, no reflection); collider none; 8k.
- `towel-drape0`: a bath towel hung over the tub's rim, butter yellow with white stripe bands, hanging nearly straight (over 60 degrees) to the floor and pooling in a rounded roll
  2-3 m high, 4.5 m out; 21 m wide, 16.5 m tall; `collider mesh`; 18k; terry pattern in the shader.
- `rubber-duck0`: the giant novelty duck, 10.5 long × 7.5 wide × 9 tall, yellow, orange beak, painted eyes (no floating eyes), looking along +z; `collider convex`; 18k.
- `toilet-roll-pyramid0`: six rolls 3-2-1, the top one a little askew, 10.8 × 3.6 × 9 m, `collider mesh`, roll gaps shut (under 0.6 m); quilted paper; 15k.
Origin at the base's centre on y = 0 (riser: at the wall mount point as documented in its header). Each file starts with a comment saying what it is and its size in metres.
`Detail detail=0.5` on big plain forms. The towel must be steeper than 60 degrees and its pooled roll's sides over 50 degrees (bots must not walk up the folds).

simplify the model or note a `Detail` change).
