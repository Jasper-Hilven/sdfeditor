# 06.11 — Detail pieces in the engine: rebuild, snapshots, budget

Part of [06](06-castles.md) step 6; rules in [06.0](06.0-rules.md). Seeing the whole castle in the level.

Depends on: 06.5, 06.10.

## Goal

`BuildAllInBatch`, commit the rebuilt assets, look at snapshots, report the cost, fix what is wrong.

## Work

1. Rebuild; commit the changed `Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Meshes/*` and `Unity/Assets/Level/**`. The pieces go through the instanced
   renderer like the other repeated parts: check that `LevelBuilder`'s prototype list includes them and that the `cull` distance and
   `shadows off` settings reach the prefab and the prototypes.
2. Snapshots from the 06.5 viewpoints, plus the courtyard at night if a night preset exists (lanterns and window panes glow), into
   `.claude/jobs/06-11-snaps/` of your worktree (not committed). Look at them.
3. A cost table as in 06's Status (draws and triangles per view, high and web). The aim for web stays 60 to 80k; say where it stands.
4. Fix what is wrong in small steps (a piece floating or too big, a count too high); re-mesh castle0 at 128 if the layout changes.

## Acceptance

- No errors in the Unity log. The table is in the report (the lead adds it to 06's Status).
- Pieces read in the snapshots from 1.5 m and are gone (culled) beyond their `cull` distance.
