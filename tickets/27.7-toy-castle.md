`tickets/done/06-castles.md` and `09-castle-fixes.md`), plus `pennant-red` and `pennant-blue`.

- 26 m along z, 19 m deep, gate at +z; walls of stacked beech and cream blocks with block crenellations, a wall-walk at 5 m, four round corner towers of cylinder

and floors tested, within 80k. Dependencies: none.
