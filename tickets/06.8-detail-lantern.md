# 06.8 — Detail piece: lantern

Part of [06](06-castles.md) step 6; rules in [06.0](06.0-rules.md). A small instanced part; placement is 06.10.

Depends on: nothing.

## Goal

`content/parts/castle/castle_lantern.sdf`: a glowing lantern in the mushroom world: a small glowing bulb (a glowing spore pod or a
small button cap turned up), about 0.25 m tall, hanging on a short curved stem hook that grows from a wall or from under a tower ring.
Origin at the hook's attachment point, so it can be placed on a wall face or under a ring; it hangs down -y, the hook reaches to +z by
0.3 m. The bulb uses a glowing material (see the keep's windows, `flower_center`, and item 5 of 06's *Detail without vertices*); the
hook a stem material. Header: `collider none`, `shadows off`, `cull 40`, `outline off`. LOD0 at most 250 triangles at 128 cells.

## Files

`content/parts/castle/castle_lantern.sdf` only.

## Commands

As [06.6](06.6-detail-window-frame.md).

## Acceptance

- The part loads and meshes within budget, reads as a hanging lantern, and the bulb is clearly the glowing material.
- Report the triangle counts per level.
