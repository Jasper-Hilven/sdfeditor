# 27.1 — Room looks: walls and lid of any size, per level (generic)

Goal: a `room` is no longer only the chessboard's checker cube. A level picks the look of its walls and ceiling, and the room may be any
size (the toy box is 150 × 64 × 100). Tickets 28-30 (kitchen, bathroom, computer) reuse this, so keep it data-driven and generic.

Steps:
1. Level format (`SDFPipeline/Levels/LevelFile.cs`, `LevelDefinition.cs`, `LevelBaker.cs`, `RoomChecker.cs`): `room size=(w, h, d)` keeps working
   unchanged (checker). New options `walls=<material> ceiling=<material>` name the materials of the room's faces (decide the folder, e.g.
   `materials/room/<name>.sdfmat`; document it in the LevelFile header comment), replacing the checker for that room. A room with `walls=` need not be
   a whole number of 8 m squares (the toy box 150 × 100 is not; check `RoomSpec.Squares` and the baked room block). Add a small generic option for an
   emissive strip along the top of one wall (27's "lid crack": a 3 m band of warm light on the north wall), e.g. `glow=north height=3 color=(...)`.
   The bake writes the look names, the sizes and the strip into `level.json`'s `room` block. Unit tests: parse, errors, bake, non-square room,
   chessboard bake unchanged.
2. Unity (`Unity/Assets/Level/Editor/LevelRoom.cs`, `Assets/Level/Shaders/SdfRoomFace.shader` or a new shader): build the walls and ceiling from the
   named materials. Write the two new materials `toybox-wall` (painted pine planks running up, 6 m wide boards with dark seams, a stencilled pastel
   alphabet frieze A-Z in yellow, green, lilac and peach as a band from 8 to 12 m up, dovetail joints drawn at the four vertical corners) and
   `toybox-lid` (darker warmer pine planks with two cross battens). The shader draws from world position (no UVs), toon bands, ink line, straight at
   eye height. The emissive strip is a quad on the top 3 m of the north wall. Colliders on all six faces as now. The chessboard room stays unchanged
   (rebuild it and compare).
3. Light (`LevelBuilder.RoomLight`): make direction, colour and ambient data-driven from the level file (a small `light` line or room options, your
   choice, documented): the chessboard keeps its overhead warm white; the toy box uses 27's values (cream-yellow, from +z towards −z pitched 50° down,
   cool lilac ambient). Walls and lid cast and take no shadows; the camera's far plane covers the room diagonal.
4. Update `LevelSnapshot`'s room views if they assume a cube. No toy box level file exists yet (27.9): test with a throwaway level file in the
   scratchpad (not committed) or a test fixture.

Check: `dotnet build`, `dotnet test` (SDFPipeline.Tests) pass; the chessboard still bakes identically. If Unity is runnable: batch
`LevelBuilder.BuildAllInBatch` (command in `tickets/done/14.2-chess-cube-unity-room.md`; first `cp -cR /Users/jasperhilven/RiderProjects/SDFProject/Unity/Library <worktree>/Unity/Library`),
no `error CS` or shader errors. Acceptance: a room with named wall and ceiling materials, of any size, bakes and builds; the chessboard is unchanged.
Dependencies: none.

## Status

Done in code and checked in Unity on throwaway levels (not the real chessboard); left for a human look.

What was done:
- Level format: `room size=(w, h, d) walls=<material> ceiling=<material>` names the faces' materials (`materials/room/<name>.sdfmat`, any size, no
  whole-squares rule, `square` not allowed with them); `glow=north|south|east|west height=3 color=(r, g, b)` adds the emissive strip along the top of
  a wall; `light=(x, y, z)` (direction towards the lamp), `lightColor=`, `ambient=` (sRGB) replace the indoor default, each optional. Documented in
  the `LevelFile` header and `RoomSpec`. `RoomSpec` gained `Walls`, `Ceiling`, `Glow`, `Light`; the checker `room size=...` line is unchanged.
  A bare value name may now hold `-` between letters and digits (`walls=toybox-wall`; `Cursor.ReadValue` in `SourceText.cs`).
- Bake: `level.json`'s `room` block has, for a materials room, `walls`/`ceiling` ({name, parameters}), per-face `extent` in metres (no `squares`,
  `dark`, `square`), and `glow` and `light` when given. A missing material names its file. The chessboard's level.json is byte-identical to the one
  baked before (diffed).
- Materials `materials/room/toybox-wall.sdfmat` and `toybox-lid.sdfmat` (paint and seam colour only).
- Unity: new `SdfRoomPlanks.shader` (boards from world position with ruled AA seams, per-board tone, stencilled 5 x 5 block alphabet A-Z in four
  pastel paints from 8 to 12 m, ink band lines, dovetail tails at the four corners with opposite rows on the two walls of a corner, two cross
  battens on the lid; no UVs, WebGL2-safe). `LevelRoom.Looks` picks the pattern by material name (a new room look adds an entry there); `LevelRoom`
  builds the faces, the glow quad (URP Unlit, Level/Room/Glow) and the six colliders as before; `CheckEdges` is skipped for a materials room.
  `LevelBuilder.RoomLightOf` reads the room's light, `RoomFarPlaneOf` makes the far plane at least 1.25 times the room diagonal (450 m kept as the least).
  `LevelSnapshot.ShootRoom` now takes the room's own size (same views for the 160 m cube) and adds `room_frieze` and `room_glow`.

Checks run: `dotnet build` 0 errors; `dotnet test SDFPipeline.Tests` 496 passed, 0 failed (new tests in `RoomTests`: parse, errors, non-square bake,
missing material, checker bake has none of the new fields, content materials). Unity batch (`BuildAllInBatch`) on two throwaway levels (a 150 x 64 x 100
materials room with glow and light; a 48 x 24 x 32 checker room) built both with no `error CS` or shader errors and the checker room's edge check passed;
snapshots of the first were taken and looked at (boards, frieze reading left to right, dovetails, lid battens, glow strip). Both throwaway levels and
their outputs were deleted.
