## Goal

`content/parts/castle/castle_veil.sdf`: the partial veil of a young mushroom as a banner: a small ring-collar hook at the top and a
tapering skirt of pale cream cloth hanging 1.2 m, 0.6 m wide at the top, with a scalloped lower edge and two or three soft folds of low
amplitude (flat faces simplify well; no `Distort` on big faces). Origin at the top middle, hanging down -y, back flat at z = 0. Material:
cream, from the existing ones (`amanita_gills` or `amanita_stem`). Read the gate's veil in `castle_wall_gate.sdf` and match its style
and thickness; this one is a small variant for walls and tower rings, so do not copy the gate's. Header: `collider none`,
`shadows off`, `cull 35`, `outline off`. LOD0 at most 300 triangles at 128 cells.
