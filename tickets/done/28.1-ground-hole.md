# 28.1 — Pipeline: a hole in the ground (the sink)

Goal: a level can cut a hole out of the ground, so the kitchen's sink is a pit in the worktop. Generic, in its own code where possible; ticket 29 (bathtub) may reuse it.

Steps:
1. Level format (`SDFPipeline/Levels/LevelFile.cs`, `LevelDefinition.cs`, `GroundRegions.cs`, `RegionMap.cs`, `GroundMesher.cs`, `LevelBaker.cs`): a region with `hole=1` (or a keyword
   of your choice, documented in the `LevelFile` header comment) leaves the ground out inside its shape: no ground triangles, no terrain collider there, the chunk edges still
   watertight round the cut. Decide how the cut stays crisp on a flat ground (cell size of the mesher; a ruled `RoundedBox2D` edge with `wobble=0`). The hole must not
   count as a region for materials or the navmesh floor.
2. Unity (`Unity/Assets/Level/Editor/*`, the terrain chunk builder): the chunk mesh and its collider follow; nothing is drawn or collidable in the hole. The navmesh baker sees no
   floor there. Keep the edit small.
3. Tests in `SDFPipeline.Tests`: parse, errors, a bake with a hole (triangles missing inside, present outside, edge of the hole inside the stated box), chunk borders crossed by a hole,
   existing levels bake byte-identical (diff `level.json` and chunk files of meadow, fungal, chessboard and toybox before and after).
4. Throwaway check: a scratch level file (scratchpad, not committed) with a 27 x 22 m hole; look at it in a snapshot if Unity runs.

Check: `dotnet build`, `dotnet test` pass. Acceptance: a hole region bakes and builds; all four existing levels are unchanged. Dependencies: none.

## Status

Done in code, tests green; Unity not run (no editor in this session).

- Level format: a region line with `hole=1` (`sink hole=1` + shapes indented below, documented in the `LevelFile` header). It is parsed into `LevelDefinition.Holes`
  (`GroundHole`, new `SDFPipeline/Levels/GroundHoles.cs`), not into `Regions`: no material, no region-map channel, not one of the four, not for `avoid=`. Only shapes are
  allowed on its line (`pattern`, `wobble` etc. are errors); a level with only holes is fine; names share the region-name check ("given twice").
- Mesher: grid points inside a hole are moved onto its edge (gradient step, as the island's edge does) and a triangle with all three corners in the hole is left out. A ruled
  edge (no noise) is therefore cut crisp to the shape at every LOD, not to the cell size; at a rounded corner a chord can cut off up to a sagitta of the arc (LOD2 cells ~0.3 m).
  `GroundMesher.Mesh` takes `GroundHoles? holes = null` as a last parameter (existing callers unchanged); `LevelScatter.Scatter` likewise, and scatters keep their `margin` clear of every hole.
- Unity: no change needed. The chunk mesh, its MeshCollider and the bots' navmesh (built from the colliders) all come from the baked glb; chunks wholly in the hole are not baked and not in level.json.
- Tests: `SDFPipeline.Tests/Levels/GroundHoleTests.cs` (14 cases: parse, errors, not counted against four regions, triangles missing inside / present outside / edge at the stated box
  for all three LODs, chunk borders crossed by the hole meet exactly, no-hole mesh unchanged, bake leaves regions.png byte-identical, scatter keeps clear).
- Commands: `dotnet build`; `dotnet test` (SDFProject.Tests 533 pass, SDFPipeline.Tests 754 pass, 0 fail); `sdf level --into` for all four levels before and after: `diff -rq` identical
  (level.json, regions.png, chunks, instances of meadow, fungal, chessboard, toybox). Scratch level (150 x 100, hole 27 x 22 at (0, 31)) bakes: 63 chunks.
- Known edge: if a hole edge lies exactly on a chunk border line (e.g. x = -12.5 on a level whose chunk lines are there), the chunk on the hole side has no triangles along it while the
  other has its border edge open; that is the hole's edge, so no crack, but the two borders do not list the same vertices. The kitchen's sink at x = +-13.5, z 20-42 does not lie on a line (lines at multiples of 25 offset by the island size) - check once the kitchen's size is fixed.
- For a human: look at the hole in a Unity snapshot once the sink model exists (rim 30 x 25 overlaps the 27 x 22 cut by 1.5 / 1.5 m); confirm the navmesh has no floor there.
