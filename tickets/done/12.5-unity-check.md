Every collider is within 1% of its render bounds (limit 20%). The collider mesh is the coarsest LOD (LOD2) of each part. The rule
"convex above 3 m becomes a mesh" holds: the two dice over 3 m are mesh colliders, the four convex ones are all under 3 m and are
solid shapes (die, d8 and chip are solid; the standing meeple's hull only fills the small notch between its legs). No convex hull
fills a hollow: `card-house`, the toppled house and `domino-chain` are all mesh.
