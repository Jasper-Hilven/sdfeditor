# 04 — Hills and island

Trees on the border hills, cheap clusters along the crests, and the rocky underside that makes the level a floating island.

Depends on: 01 (hills, edge), 03 (instanced renderer, wind).

## Trees

From 1.5 m eye height the hill crests (8–12 m) plus the first row of trees hide everything behind them. All trees are **instanced**
through the `InstancedScatterRenderer` from 03 — no tree GameObjects with renderers or LOD groups in the level scene. Draws then
depend on variants × parts × LODs (roughly 50–80 for all trees), not on the number of trees; ~100 tree prefabs with their own
renderers and outlines would cost several hundred draws, far over the integrated-GPU / web budget of ≤ 200.

- **Front row**: 80–120 trees from the 18 variants (`content/models/trees/*0..2`) on the inner slopes and the crest, placed by
  `sdf level` (new `trees` block in the level file: allowed variants with weights, density, spacing ≈ 5–8 m, a band from the
  rectangle's edge to the crest) and written to `instances.bin` in draw group `trees`.
- **Crest clusters**: 8–12 new models in `content/models/trees/clusters/`, each 4–8 existing canopy parts (`content/parts/*canopy*`,
  `*crown*`) and trunks smooth-unioned into one blob mass, 2–5k triangles at LOD0, `--lods 3`. Placed along and behind the crest
  (≈ 150 instances), draw group `trees` (or a `clusters` group with coarser rings).
- **Tree rings** (trees stay visible across the whole level, up to ≈ 110 m away; their outlines carry the hill silhouette):

  | Distance | Level | Outline | Shadows |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0–25 m | LOD0 | yes | cast |
  | 25–60 m | LOD1 | yes | cast (high preset only) |
  | > 60 m | LOD2 | yes | no |

  Never culled by distance inside the level, only by the frustum. Integrated GPU / web: distances × 0.7, shadows only from LOD0.
- Prototypes come from the tree prefabs `MushroomBuilder` already builds (meshes per part and LOD); the renderer reads their meshes,
  it does not instantiate the prefabs.
- No impostors (they clash with toon lighting and outlines) and no skyline meshes (the crest hides the horizon).
- Canopy wind: the draw group's wind setting in the shared vertex sway from 03, weaker than grass, weighted by height above the
  trunk's base so trunks stay put.

## Tree collision

- The pipeline writes a **trunk capsule** per tree model into the glb extras (fitted from the trunk part: base, top, radius), using
  the per-model collider setting from 02 (`collider capsule`).
- `LevelBuilder` writes, for each tree instance in reach of the player (front row), a **static collider-only GameObject** with that
  `CapsuleCollider`, at the instance's position, yaw and scale — no renderer. Canopies get no collider.
- Crest clusters get one capsule or box each (they are behind the invisible walls; just enough for physics objects), the same way.

## Island underside

- Behind the crest, the island ends at an edge (`edge margin` in the level file). Below it: a baked rocky underside, a large SDF
  model (`content/models/terrain/island-underside.sdf` or generated by `sdf level` from the edge outline: a downward cone/blob with
  noise and a few hanging rock spurs), coarse (≈ 10–20k triangles), no outline, no shadow casting, materials from
  `materials/rocks/` plus a soil band at the top.
- It meets the terrain's outer edge with no visible gap from any reachable viewpoint (players rarely see it; it should still make
  sense from a high vantage point like a castle tower).

## Sky

Sky only beyond the island. The sky is ticket [07](07-sky.md): presets per level, the meadow's Eye-Moon Night with its glowing abyss
below the island and rising spores, and the toon tint and distance fade. This ticket adds nothing to the sky. The underside must look
right under 07's night preset (tower and edge snapshots), and it sits in front of 07's abyss spores.

## Acceptance

- From anywhere in the play area, the border reads as a continuous wooded hill line; no gaps showing sky between trunks at eye
  height, no visible edge of the world.
- From a castle tower, the island edge and underside look intentional.
- Trees block the player where reachable; canopies do not.
- No tree in the level scene is a rendered GameObject; all are drawn by the instanced renderer.
- Trees cost ≤ 400k triangles on the high preset, ≤ 120k on integrated GPU / web, and ≤ 80 draws including outlines.
