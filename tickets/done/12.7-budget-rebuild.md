## Result

Done, without the budget part. `sdf level content/levels/chessboard.level --into Unity/Assets/Level/Generated/chessboard` baked 100
chunks and 130 placed models (the 93 chess pieces and the 37 game pieces) with no warning. Note the ticket's command above is wrong:
`--into` is the level's own folder, so with `Generated` as in the Commands block the bake lands loose beside the `chessboard/` and
`meadow/` folders and Unity builds a scene with only the 93 chess pieces. I did that once, saw "93 placed models", deleted the loose
files and baked again into `.../Generated/chessboard`.

`LevelBuilder.BuildAllInBatch` (Unity 6000.3.0f1, batch mode, the main checkout) built `Chessboard.unity` with 130 placed models, 100
chunks, 69,269 ground triangles at LOD0, 2,807 scattered; no `error CS`, shader error, exception or warning from the level builder in
the logs. The build only gave the right picture after one more step, though: the committed chess prefabs (`pawn-dark.prefab` and the
other 18) pointed at mesh assets that do not exist on this machine, because the prefabs were built earlier than the current meshes
and Unity's staleness check (prefab newer than glb) did not catch it. The first snapshots showed the game pieces and no chess piece at
all (placed instances with zero-size bounds). I rebuilt the prefabs of `chess/` and `games/` (56 models) with
`MushroomBuilder.Build` and the level again; then the chess pieces are there. That also rewrote the guids in those tracked prefabs;
they are machine-specific and not committed. A fresh clone does not hit this, as its checked-out prefabs are older than the glbs
`sdf unity` makes and so rebuild.

Which models: the chessboard takes `{model}.prefab`, and the builder picks the `_512` variant of a model when its glb exists.
Local `_512` glbs exist for the chess pieces, grass, flowers and more, so for the build and the snapshots I moved all 158 `_512` glb
and meta files under `Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Generated/` to `/private/tmp/claude-501/ticket12.7/aside/` and put the 79 glbs back
afterwards; so everything seen below is what a fresh clone gets (256 cells, 3 LODs). One catch: the nine `_512` glbs of the game
models that 12.5 saw next to the 256 ones were no longer in `Generated/games/` when I started the build (not moved by me; a
`ls` a minute before listed them), so they are gone; they were git-ignored, never meshed by this ticket, and `sdf unity` makes only
256 anyway. Nothing was meshed at 512.

Snapshots (1920 x 1080, High quality level, standing at 1.5 m eye height, from a throwaway editor script that was deleted again;
`LevelSnapshot` was not changed, its crowd shot picks the densest spot of all placed models, which is a chess cluster) are in
`/private/tmp/claude-501/ticket12.7/`, not in the repo:

- `chess_close.png`, `chess_mid.png`, `corner_across.png`, `corner_across_high.png`, `top.png`: with the chess pieces in.
  `corner_across_high.png` is from the board's corner across all of it, `top.png` is straight down at 230 m.
- `game-closeups/`: dice spill, house street, house of cards, domino run, chip stack, meeples, with eye-height and from-above views.
  Taken in the first pass, so no chess piece is in them (the broken chess prefabs above); the game pieces are as in the final
  scene (same placements, same prefabs). `top_without_chess.png` and the two `corner_across..._without_chess.png` are the same views
  as the first three above before the chess prefabs were fixed.

What I saw, honestly: every game piece I looked at stands on the board. Nothing floats and nothing is sunk, the soft shadows sit
under each piece: the three dice (the d8 resting on a facet, the two cubes on edges and faces), the houses (red and teal caps, the
toppled one lying on its side with its cap to the camera), the hotel, the domino run (one leaning on the next, one on its long side,
one standing), the poker chip stack with five chips and the rolling chip on its edge, the house of cards, the card face down and the
card stuck in the ground at an angle, both meeples (one upright, one fallen). The orientation is right (houses roof up, dominoes
with pips on the large face, card backs towards the red side, a stack of chips flat). The pieces are small against a 160 m board:
from the corner only the hotel, the houses and the dice read at 60-80 m; the chips, cards and meeples are specks there, and in
`top.png` the game pieces are hard to tell from the chess pieces. The cards and dominoes look flat white with little pattern at
distance, and the board looks sparse more than chaotic from the corner; that is a placement or scale judgement for a human, not a
fault. All 130 pieces show; I did not look at each of the 37 up close (the rest only from a distance in the corner and top shots). The prefabs' colliders and the exact fit to square lines were not checked (all in 12.6's overlap test only).
