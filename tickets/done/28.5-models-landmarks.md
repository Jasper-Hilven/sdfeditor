- Deviations from the table: (1) the burners are 0.16 m thick, not 0.02 m (a 0.02-0.04 m sheet does not mesh at 256 cells, or costs 48k triangles; 0.16 m is a 2.7 mm real coil); no collider, so nothing blocks a walk over it. (2) The heat parameter is NOT in the model: the vertex streams and materials have no heat or emission yet (30.1 adds emission); the burners use the one material `kitchen_burner` so 28.10 can write heat to exactly those vertices. (3) The rolling pin's axis is 0.55 m up and the pin lies along z (placed with yaw 90 it spans the sink): tip top 1.15 m, middle top 2.05 m (table: 2.15), the middle hangs 0.95 m below y = 0. (4) The toppled cereal box is 14.4 m long and 12 m high, the spilled cereal brings the whole model to 18 m (table: 18 x 4.2 x 12); the flaps and spill make it about 6 m deep. (5) Kettle is 9.6 m long along x (spout to handle side, spout on +x), about 9.3 m across z; blender 8.6 m wide with its handle on +x; tap's spout points to -z (place it north of the sink with no yaw). (6) The sink's bowl is 13.2 m below y = 0, steel 0.5 m thick, 0.45 m of water as a flat puddle (an `Ellipsoid` there made the bounds finder fail), suds heap cut flat on the floor.
- Every model was rendered (`sdf render --style toon`) and looked at. Fixes from the pngs: the tap's and kettle's handle chains were lumpy from `SmoothUnion` (plain union of more capsules, one a zero-length capsule that made the finder return nothing: NaN); the suds spheres poked through the sink's floor (intersected with a box above it); the water ellipsoid broke the finder (a `RoundedCylinder` puddle); the pin was a diamond with a crease (an ellipsoid cut by a rounded box); the pepper mill's head was far too wide (two rebuilds); the cake and fruit colours were too close (golden sponge, fruit moved up and a third-layer orange).
