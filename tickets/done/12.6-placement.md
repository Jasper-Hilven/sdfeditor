# 12.6 — Place the game pieces, with an overlap check

Part of [12](12-game-pieces.md).

## Goal

The game pieces in `content/levels/chessboard.level` (the block in 12), with no piece sunk into a chess piece or another game piece
beyond the intended chaos, checked by a test.

## Files

- `content/levels/chessboard.level`: the `place` block at the comment "The game elements ... go here" (about line 249); put the block
  from 12 in place of the placeholder comment as a start.
- Test: `LevelFileTests.ContentChessboard_...` (find it with `grep -rn ContentChessboard --include=*.cs .`): extend it and add
  `ContentChessboard_GamePieces_DoNotOverlapEachOtherOrTheChessPieces`.
- Model sizes: each model's footprint from its `collider` line or the glb bounds (`Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Generated/games/`, `.../chess/`),
  scaled by the placement's `scale`; circles on the ground (centre `at`, radius = half the larger footprint side times scale) is enough.
- Regenerated: `Unity/Assets/Level/Generated/chessboard/`.
- This ticket file: Result section (the final list of changed placements).

## Commands

```
dotnet build SDFPipeline.Cli -c Release
SDFPipeline.Cli/bin/Release/net10.0/sdf level content/levels/chessboard.level --into Unity/Assets/Level/Generated
dotnet test
```

## Rules for the overlap check

- Game pieces of one intended group (the dice spill, the house street, `card-house` with its cards, the domino run) may touch but
  not interpenetrate by more than 25% of the smaller radius. Pieces of different groups, and all game pieces against chess pieces:
  no overlap of the footprint circles. Take the chess pieces from their own placements in the file.
- When the check fails, move the game piece (never a chess piece) by the smallest step, or lower its scale (not below 0.8), or drop
  it. Keep every game piece on the board (|x|, |z| < 76) and every model name valid.
- Keep the set varied: all 20 models used at least once.

## Acceptance

- The block is in the level file, all 20 models used, all placements on the board.
- The new test passes and fails if a game piece is moved onto a chess piece (try it once by hand).
- `sdf level` bakes without a warning, deterministic; `dotnet build` and `dotnet test` pass. Regenerate (never hand-edit) the baked
  level files under `Unity/Assets/Level/Generated/chessboard/` and commit them.

## Result

All 37 placements of the block in 12 are in `content/levels/chessboard.level`, replacing the placeholder comment, and all 20 game
models are used (dice 7, houses and hotels 7, dominoes 6, meeples 5, chips 4, cards 8 placements). Every one is on the board
(|x|, |z| < 76). The comments in the block now each start a group (the dice spill, the crooked street, the domino run, the meeple pair,
the chip stack with its runaway, the house of cards with its spilled hand); the strays have a comment of their own each, so they
count as groups of one.

The test `LevelFileTests.ContentChessboard_GamePieces_DoNotOverlapEachOtherOrTheChessPieces` takes each placement's footprint from the
model's bounds (`LevelModels.In`), puts a circle on the bounds' centre, turned and scaled as placed, with half the larger side as radius,
and fails for any game piece that sinks into another game piece or a chess piece. Pieces of one comment group may sink by a quarter of
the smaller radius, everything else not at all. The first run found six overlaps, none of which was a chess piece moved; five game
pieces and then two again were moved, by a few metres each (the new places are in the file; no scale was lowered, none dropped):

- `monopoly-hotel` from (37.2, -50.3) to (39.2, -48.6): it stood on a knight.
- `monopoly-hotel` from (-58.1, 60.5) via (-60, 52) to (-69, 36): both spots lay inside the dense chess pile at the left edge; it now
  stands beside the other house of that corner.
- `domino-6-3-lying` from (-13.2, 1.8) to (-12.5, -2.0): it stood on a pawn.
- `poker-chip-stack` from (10.3, -21.4) via (14.5, -24.5) to (4.0, -28.5): a pawn on top of it, then the next pawn.
- `card-three-lying` from (-4.3, 57.6) to (-1.0, 61.5): it was under a pawn.

Two more tests: `..._GamePieces_UseAllTwentyModelsOnTheBoard`, and `..._OverlapCheck_FindsAGamePieceMovedOntoAChessPiece`, which moves a
domino onto a chess piece and expects the check to complain. I also did it by hand once: a meeple moved onto the rook at
(-24.5, 22.9) made the test fail with two overlaps (2.25 m and 0.64 m), then I put it back. The old placement-count range in
`ContentChessboard_IsAFlatCheckerboard_...` went from 60-120 to 60-160 (now 130).

`dotnet build` has no errors or warnings, `dotnet test` passes (533 + 354). `sdf level` bakes without a warning into
`Unity/Assets/Level/Generated/chessboard/` (130 placed models, 2,807 scattered); those files are git-ignored, so there is nothing of
them in the commit. The circles are rough for long thin pieces (dominoes, fallen chess pieces), so two pieces that look apart may still
count as overlapping, never the other way round. Left for 12.7: rebake in Unity, rebuild `Chessboard.unity`, and the triangle budget
with the 37 new pieces; and a look at the board in Unity to judge the spread by eye.
