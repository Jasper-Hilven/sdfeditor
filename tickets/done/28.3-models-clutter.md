# 28.3 — Models: worktop clutter

Goal: the `crumbs` layer's small things of 28's "Ground clutter" and "New models list" tables, as SDF models with materials, rendered, bounds-tested and meshed. Models in `content/models/kitchen/`,
parts in `content/parts/kitchen/`, materials in `materials/kitchen/`. Palette: honey, cream, mustard, lemon, mint, sage, cocoa, apricot, lilac, soft pink, butter yellow, steel; no scarlet, no blue.

Models (sizes and LOD0 budgets from 28's tables): `crumb0..2`, `rice-grain0..1`, `cereal-loop0..2` (honey, cocoa, mint tints), `coffee-bean0..1`, `sprinkle0..3` (yellow, mint, pink, white),
`sugar-drift0..1` (low mound of crystals; sparkle from the shader or a few facets), `pea0..1` (one and three together). Collider none.
Sizes are 60 times life size. Origin at the base's centre on y = 0. Each file starts with a comment that says what it is and its size in metres. Big simple forms take `Detail detail=0.5`; no noise on big faces.

Steps: write .sdf files and materials; `sdf render` each and LOOK at every png (Read it): is it what it should be, any red or blue? Fix. Bounds tests in `SDFPipeline.Tests/Models/` (size, base on y = 0).
Mesh at 256 with 3 LODs (`sdf mesh` for just these) and check LOD0 triangle counts against budgets; list them in your report.

Check: `dotnet test` passes. Acceptance: every model rendered and looked at, within budget, tests pass. Dependencies: none.
