# 12.4 — Part split check and triangle table

Part of [12](../12-game-pieces.md). Reads what 12.1 to 12.3 recorded; fixes what is wrong in the models.

## Goal

For all 20 models: the meshing outcome understood and written down in 12's text (table of triangles per LOD), the part-split
questions of 12 item 2 answered, and fixes made where a result is wrong.

## Files

- Input: the "Result" sections of `12.1-mesh-cards.md`, `12.2-mesh-dice-houses-chips.md`, `12.3-remesh-existing-at-256.md`; the glbs in
  `Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Generated/games/`.
- Possibly edited: `content/models/games/die-*.sdf`, `content/parts/die_pips.sdf`, `content/models/games/monopoly-hotel.sdf`,
  `content/parts/porthole_window.sdf`.
- Edited: `tickets/12-game-pieces.md` (a "Meshed" section with the table and the findings).

## What to check

1. **die_pips**: used once under a `SmoothUnion`, so meshed in place as a second mesh overlapping the body (no fillet). Render with
   `sdf render` (see the CLI help for the options; a close-up of a die) and look at the toon image: if the pips look loose or
   doubled, inline them into the die models (replace the `Part part=die_pips` line by its content) and re-mesh just those dice at
   256 (`sdf mesh content/models/games/<die>.sdf Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Generated/games/<die>.glb --resolution 256 --lods 3`).
2. **Instanced parts**: for `poker-chip-stack` (chips), `monopoly-hotel` / `monopoly-house` (portholes) and the hotel's eyes the log
   says "instanced" or "meshed in place ... across the mirror of a SymmetryX". Where a SymmetryX crossing is the cause, move the
   part so each use stays on its side of the mirror if easy (see 11 "Known problems" 2); otherwise leave it and say so.
3. **Painted models are one mesh**: cards and chips have no second mesh lying on the same surface (z-fight). Count the meshes per
   glb (a short script reading the glb JSON chunk, or the log); a card is one mesh (plus hands and eyes for `card-ace-held`).
4. **Triangles**: the table, per model: LOD0, LOD1, LOD2 triangles and the glb size. Flag any model over 60k at LOD0.

## Acceptance

- `tickets/12-game-pieces.md` holds the table and a short finding per check above.
- Every model with a change has a fresh 256 glb; `dotnet build` and `dotnet test` pass.
