Subticket of [31 — Story intro](31-story-intro.md). Goal: panels get their balloons, captions, SFX lettering, speed lines, bursts and borders, drawn at capture time.

Steps:
1. In the capture (editor only), after the post pass, draw per panel from its data: panel border (thick ink, slightly irregular),
   inset frames with gutters, speech balloons with tails pointing at the tail target, caption boxes (cream, ink outline, as the GUI
   panel style in `Unity/Assets/GUI/`), Prime's jagged blue sky box, SFX lettering in Lilita One over an impact burst, radial or
   parallel speed lines, motion lines. Use TextMesh Pro for the text (fonts under `Unity/Assets/GUI/Fonts`), rendered into the panel
   image.

   safe area of about 5 px from the panel edge (the GUI edge margin).
3. Keep the layout code pure where possible (balloon placement, text fitting) and add EditMode tests under `Unity/Assets/Story/Tests/`
   for it (follow `Unity/Assets/Battle/Tests/`).

Check: Unity compiles; panels 1, 5 and 11 render with lettering; look at them. Dependencies: 31.5.
