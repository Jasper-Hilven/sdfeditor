# 28.7 — Models: the kitchen room, the island base and the worktop edge

Goal: the backdrop models of 28 ("The room", "Footprint"): `kitchen-room`, `counter-base`, `worktop-edge`, as SDF models with materials, rendered, bounds-tested and meshed, so the form from 28.2 can use them.
They are big and plain: the budget is 40k triangles and 8 materials or fewer in all for `kitchen-room` (128 cells if 256 is too heavy; 3 LODs), no noise on large faces.

- `kitchen-room` (300 x 156 x 270; floor 55 m below the worktop, ceiling 101 m above): grey slate floor, cream walls, white metro tile band to 36 m above the north counter, north wall run of counters
  with a 72 x 72 m sash window (daylight, a basil pot on the sill), upper cabinets beside it, west and east walls counters and upper cabinets with warm under-cabinet light strips, south wall a tall cream fridge
  (a few magnets, no "42") and the door, two brass pendant lamps with shades 65 m above the worktop. Strips, lamps and window are emissive: check how the toon shader reads emission per vertex
  (27.1's glow strip, the other levels) and use that route; do not add lights. The model's origin is the worktop's surface centre, so the level can place it at (0, 0, 0).
- `counter-base` (146 x 96 x 55): the island's sage green cabinet base set 3 m back under the worktop, brass handles, plinth at the floor.
- `worktop-edge`: the 2.4 m thick oak slab's rounded edge round the play area plus a 1 m lip (152 x 102 m outside, the 150 x 100 m play area inside; a frame piece only so the ground is not duplicated), top at y = 0.
  Its inner edge meets the ground at y = 0 without a seam.
Materials in `materials/kitchen/`. Palette as 28.

Steps: write, render and LOOK at every png (from the worktop looking out at the room is the key view: render with a camera on the worktop if the CLI allows), bounds tests, mesh, report triangles.

Check: `dotnet test` passes. Acceptance: renders looked at, `kitchen-room` within 40k and 8 materials, tests pass. Dependencies: none (28.2 for the form that uses them).
