# 31.2 — Home models: props and the crown

Subticket of [31 — Story intro](31-story-intro.md). Goal: the props that carry the story exist as meshed SDF models in `content/models/home/`.

Steps:
1. Model: `snack-bowl` (0.25 m, crisps as 5-6 separate small pieces, each its own part so the capture can toss them), `crisp-bag`
   (0.2 m, crumpled), `soda-can` (0.12 m), `shelf` (1 m, two brackets), `trophy` (0.4 m, a golden minigun on a cap-shaped base with a
   "1st" plate; the brightest gold in the room), `shelf-stuff` (3-4 small things: books, a game box, a plush blob), `lamp` (0.6 m
   desk lamp, cap-shaped shade, emissive shade material), `rug` (1.5 m round), `poster` (0.5 x 0.7 m flat quad with UVs; its picture is
   a texture later) and `prime-crown` (gold, five points, blue gems, sized to sit slightly too small on the chanterelle scout's cap;
   if ticket 33 already has a crown model use that and skip this one).
2. Reuse 31.1's home materials; add any missing one in the same family.
3. Mesh at 256 and extend the pipeline test from 31.1 so every model of the list above parses, builds and has its named parts.

Check: `dotnet build` and `dotnet test` pass; the models mesh. Dependencies: 31.1.
