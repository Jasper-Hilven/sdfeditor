# 06.4 — Castle materials with patterns, assigned to the parts

Part of [06](06-castles.md) step 5; rules in [06.0](06.0-rules.md). Gives the castle its wall stones, stem fibres and shelf gills.

Depends on: 06.1 (the `pattern` line must parse).

## Goal

New materials in `materials/buildings/`, drafts in `06-new/`: `castle_wall.sdfmat` (stones 0.55 / 0.035), `castle_stem.sdfmat`
(fibres 0.08 / 0.012), `castle_shelf.sdfmat` (gills 0.09 / 0.012). Same colours as the materials they replace (`amanita_stem`,
`amanita_gills`), so the castle looks the same plus the pattern. Assign: wall bays and the gate bay's wall `castle_wall`; tower, keep,
turret and joint stems `castle_stem`; the stairs' shelf fungi `castle_shelf`. Not caps, and not the small details (amanitas, mycelium,
merlons). Rule: a pattern goes where the surface is large and plain.

Mind ticket 09's cap/stem region logic: a new material name is another region, so re-check the cap/stem borders at LOD2 (render the
tower per level, as 09 did) for red on stems.

## Files

`materials/buildings/*.sdfmat` (3 new), `content/parts/castle/*.sdf` (material names only), regenerated
`Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Generated/buildings/castle0.glb`.

## Commands

Find how castle0.glb is made (likely `sdf mesh content/models/buildings/castle0.sdf --into Unity/Assets/Mushrooms/Generated --resolution 128 --lods 3`;
check the run configs and 09's Status for the exact form and output folder), run it, `sdf render` a tower and a bay.
`dotnet build`, `dotnet test`.

## Acceptance

- castle0.glb has the three materials with their patterns in `extras.sdf.materials`; geometry unchanged (same triangle counts per
  part, or say why not).
- No cap colour on stems at any level. `dotnet test` passes.
