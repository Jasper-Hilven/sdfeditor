`IsUnlocked`/`NextStep`, loss streak, `RecordWin`), `Medals.Of` (section 6), `Difficulty.Apply(setup)` (Easy/Hard rules, clamps,
RETRY EASIER offsets). Beat text is data too, but its type can wait for 32.7.

Tests: the order, unlocks after each win, New Campaign keeping medals, a broken save, medals at their edges, difficulty clamps, a JSON
round trip, an unavailable step being skipped by `NextStep` yet reachable once it is marked available.
