- **Patterns**: the toon shader has only stones, fibres and gills patterns, so the sponge pores, scrub pits, bath bomb sprinkles and terry loops are the materials' speckle parameters (baked per vertex), and the toilet paper's quilting is `pattern stones size=0.22 grout=0.008` (blocks with inked joints). Neither shows in `sdf render` (patterns are not drawn there; speckles are), so check both in Unity.
- **Gotchas found**: `Plane` is `dot(p, n) + height`, so a top cut at y = 3 is `normal=(0,1,0) height=-3`; and a pair of `Plane` cuts inside an `Intersection` made `SdfBoundsFinder` (and so render and mesh) lose the whole body of the toilet roll, so the rolls cut with a `Box` instead. `RoundedCylinder radius` is the total radius (roundness is inside it). `SmoothUnion smoothness` inflates a shape by about that much. `SdfBoundsFinder` is too coarse to size a model by (whole octree cells, 4 m default box), so the test measures on a 5 cm grid inside the finder's box (about 40 s for the file).
- Every model rendered (`sdf render --style toon`) and looked at.
