# 06.7 — Detail piece: shelf fungi on walls

Part of [06](06-castles.md) step 6; rules in [06.0](06.0-rules.md). A small instanced part; placement is 06.10.

Depends on: nothing.

## Goal

`content/parts/castle/castle_wall_shelf.sdf`: a cluster of two or three bracket/shelf fungi stepping up a wall face (the biggest about
0.6 m wide and 0.12 m thick, fanned half discs with a rounded lip, a paler underside in `amanita_gills`, a cap tone from the existing
mushroom materials). Modelled at its own origin, back plane at z = 0 (so it can sit 4 cm into a wall), growing toward +z. The cluster
is about 0.8 m wide and 1.0 m high. Decorative only: `collider none`, `shadows off`, `cull 35`, `outline off`. LOD0 at most 400
triangles at 128 cells.

## Files

`content/parts/castle/castle_wall_shelf.sdf` only.

## Commands

As [06.6](06.6-detail-window-frame.md): a scratch model using the part, `sdf mesh … --resolution 128 --lods 3` for counts, `sdf render`
front and side views. `dotnet build`, `dotnet test`.

## Acceptance

- The part loads and meshes within budget, reads as shelf fungi in the render, and does not float (flat back for sitting on a wall).
- Report the triangle counts per level.
